Sortshot Privacy Policy

App: Sortshot: Screenshot Organizer (Android) · Developer: SM Technologies · Effective date: October 20, 2026

In short
  • Your screenshots are read and organized on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • We never collect the text in your screenshots, your searches, file names or anything the app finds in them.
  • The app sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports through Google Firebase. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no account, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. What the app does on your phone

Sortshot finds the screenshots on your phone, reads the text in them with on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit), and builds a search index, categories, extracted details (such as amounts, dates, links and phone numbers) and a duplicate finder. All of this happens on your device. Your screenshots, their text and the search index stay in the app's private storage on your phone and are not sent to us or anyone else.

2. Permissions

  • Photos and images: to find and read your screenshots. You can allow all photos or only selected ones.
  • Notifications: to show progress while screenshots are being indexed or exported.
  • Foreground service: to finish an export you started, even if you leave the app.
  • Internet: only for the anonymous stats and crash reports described below. It is not used for your screenshots.

3. Data we collect

If "Share anonymous app stats" is on in Settings, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix crashes and understand which features are used. This is what may be sent:

TypeExamplesPurpose
App activityWhich screens are opened; a fixed list of events such as "photo access granted", "export started", "app data deleted"Improve the app
Crash logs and diagnosticsCrash reports, short fixed error codes (for example "text recognition failed"), device model, Android version, app versionFix bugs
Approximate locationCountry or city, worked out by Firebase from a masked IP address (never GPS)See where the app is used
Device or other IDsA random app instance ID created by Firebase (not your advertising ID)Count users and sessions without knowing who you are

We never send screenshot images, text found in screenshots, search words, file names, extracted details, collection names or any other content you create. Usage stats are off in debug and test builds.

The app does not use the Android advertising ID, does not show ads and does not personalize ads. Firebase may process your IP address to deliver the data; Google states it does not store full IP addresses for Analytics.

4. Your choices

  • Turn off stats: Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous app stats". Turning it off stops collection and clears the stats stored on your phone.
  • Delete app data: Settings → "Delete all app data" removes the search index, collections, pins and settings. Your screenshots stay in your gallery.
  • Uninstall: removes all app data from your phone.

5. Private vault

Screenshots you move to the vault are encrypted and stored only inside the app on your phone, behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock. They are not backed up. If you uninstall the app or delete its data, vaulted screenshots are lost, so restore them to your gallery first.

6. Backups

The search index, settings and vault are excluded from Android cloud backup. Your original screenshots are handled by your phone's own gallery and backup settings, not by Sortshot.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except Google acting as our service provider for Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. When you choose to share a screenshot from the app, it goes only to the app you pick.

8. Data retention

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 2 months, then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

9. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox, and vaulted screenshots are additionally encrypted.

10. Children

Sortshot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
