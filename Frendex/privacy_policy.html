Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Frendex ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application Frendex (the "Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide:

  • Account Credentials: Full Name, Email Address, and encrypted Password.
  • Profile Information: Profile picture/avatar (optional).
  • Expense & Ledger Data: Group titles, expense amounts, descriptions, categories, split shares, and payment settlements.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Device Data:

  • Usage & Analytics Data: Anonymous app interaction events collected via Google Firebase Analytics to improve application stability and performance.
  • Network & Device Information: Operating system version and network connectivity status (used for managing offline synchronization queues).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Creating and maintaining your user account.
  • Calculating shared group balances, debts, and settlement ledgers.
  • Enabling real-time and offline synchronization across your devices.
  • Sending essential in-app notifications regarding new expenses, edits, and payments.
  • Improving Application stability, security, and user experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Database Security: All user data is securely stored using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure) with enterprise-grade encryption (TLS/SSL in transit and AES-256 at rest).
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Strict database access control policies ensure that only authorized group members or direct friends can view shared expense records.
  • Offline Security: Local data cached for offline functionality is stored securely inside your device's browser IndexedDB container.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure providers (Supabase for backend database hosting, Firebase for anonymous app performance analytics).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or legal process to protect the safety, property, or legal rights of our users or the public.

5. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have full control over your personal information:

  • In-App Controls: You can edit or delete your account profile and expenses directly within the Application.
  • Data Removal Request: To request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data from our servers, send an email to smtechnologies.dev@gmail.com. Upon request, all user credentials and linked records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
