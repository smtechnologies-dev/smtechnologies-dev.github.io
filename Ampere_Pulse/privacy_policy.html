Privacy Policy for Ampere Pulse

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ampere Pulse ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a comprehensive battery monitoring experience, we collect certain non-personal device information:

  • Battery Diagnostics: Real-time current (mA), voltage, temperature, health status, and charging cycles.
  • Device Metadata: Device manufacturer, model name, and Android OS version.

This information is processed locally on your device to display diagnostics and is not stored on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to show relevant ads. This data may include:

  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Approximate Location (IP-based)
  • App interaction data

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions to function:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show real-time battery status in your notification tray.
  • INTERNET: To load advertisements via AdMob.

4. Data Security

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. All battery-related data remains on your device and is cleared when the App is closed or uninstalled.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
