MotionAlign: Car Sickness Dots

Last updated: October 6, 2026

MotionAlign: Car Sickness Dots (the "App") is developed by SM Tech Studio ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information our app accesses, why it is needed, and how it is handled. By using our App, you agree to the terms described here.

1. Information Processed Only on Your Device

The motion cues themselves run entirely on your device:

  • Motion sensors: The App reads your device's motion sensors (accelerometer, and where available the system's gravity and linear-acceleration sensors) to detect vehicle motion such as turning, braking and accelerating. This calculation happens only on your device. Sensor readings are never saved or transmitted.
  • Display over other apps: Used to draw the motion dots on top of your other apps. The dots do not read or capture what is on your screen.
  • Foreground service: Keeps the motion cues running while you use other apps.
  • Notifications: Shows an ongoing notification while the cues are running, with a Stop button so you can turn them off at any time.
  • Settings: Your preferences (dot size, color, layout, sensitivity) are stored only on your device.

Note: We do not operate our own servers or databases, and we never collect your motion data, your location history, or the content of other apps.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to support ongoing development. Where you have consented (or where consent is not required), ads may be personalized; otherwise non-personalized ads are shown. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), and ad interaction and diagnostic data, in order to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This data is processed and shared by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tools. You can opt out of personalized advertising on your device at any time (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how the App's features are used and to fix crashes. These services collect:

  • An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase
  • Device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • App interactions, for example opening the App, granting permissions, starting or stopping the cues, and changing settings
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information

We do not collect your name, email, contacts or precise location through these services, and motion sensor readings are never included. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase's default retention settings and processed under Firebase's privacy and security terms.

4. Data Security and Deletion

Data sent to Google AdMob and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Because we do not hold any account or personal profile for you, we cannot identify you from this data. You can request deletion of analytics data associated with your app installation by emailing us (see Contact Us); uninstalling the App also stops all further collection and removes your locally stored settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or security improvements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
