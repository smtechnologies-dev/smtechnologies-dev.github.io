LinkSilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy explains how LinkSilo ("we", "us") handles your data. It covers the LinkSilo Android app and the LinkSilo browser extension (Chrome and Chromium-based browsers).

Questions or requests: smtechnologies.dev@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

A. Using LinkSilo without an account (guest mode)

Your links, titles, descriptions, categories, tags, notes and reminders are stored only on your device (app) or in your browser (extension). They are not uploaded to our servers.

Even in guest mode, the Android app uses Google services that collect some technical data (see Analytics and Ads). To show a preview of a link you save, the app contacts that website (and, for some sites, its public preview service such as TikTok or Instagram embeds) and requests a site icon from Google's favicon service, which receives the site's domain.

B. If you sign in (optional, for sync)

Signing in with Google turns on sync between your phone and the browser extension. We then collect and store:

DataPurpose
Name, email address, profile picture and account ID (from Google Sign-In)Create and identify your account
Your saved links: URL, title, description, preview image, site icon and domain; categories, tags, favorite/archived/read status, Private Folder flag, AI summaries, notes and reminder timesSync your library across your devices
Number of AI summaries used per dayEnforce daily limits
Subscription statusUnlock Pro features

C. Analytics (Android app)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. It collects app interactions (for example screens viewed, links saved, sign-in, ad events and errors), an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, approximate location derived from IP address, and, after you sign in, your LinkSilo account ID. We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings (such as how often ads appear) without an update.

D. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment card details. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and your LinkSilo account ID to verify your subscription. We store whether your account is Pro and until when.

E. Device features

  • Fingerprint / screen lock for the Private Folder is handled entirely by Android. We never receive or store biometric data.
  • Notifications are used only for reminders you set; they are scheduled on your device.
  • Photos/media: only used if you choose to save a QR code image to your gallery.

3. AI summaries

AI summaries are available to signed-in users. When you request one, our server receives the link's URL, title and description, downloads the public web page, and sends that page text to Google's Gemini API to generate the summary. The summary is saved with the link.

Google may use content sent through the Gemini API to provide and improve its services, under Google's own terms. Do not request summaries of pages you consider sensitive.

4. Ads and consent

The free version of the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and where required elsewhere), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Pro subscribers see no ads.

The browser extension shows no ads.

6. Browser extension

The extension only acts when you use it. It does not track your browsing and contains no analytics or ads.

  • activeTab, tabs and the content script: read the current page's title, URL, description, preview image and icon only when you click save.
  • contextMenus: "Save to LinkSilo" right-click options.
  • storage: keep guest links and your sign-in session in your browser.
  • notifications: confirm saves made from the right-click menu.
  • identity: sign in with Google.

Guest links stay in your browser. When you are signed in, saved links go to your LinkSilo account (Supabase) and sync with the app. All extension code is bundled in the package; no remote code is executed.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our providers, who encrypt data at rest. Each account can only access its own data. The Private Folder hides links behind your device's fingerprint or screen lock; it is a privacy feature, not encryption. No method of storage is 100% secure.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a link, note, tag or reminder permanently removes it from our database the next time your device syncs.
  • Deleting your account permanently deletes your account and all cloud data, including links, notes, tags, reminders, AI usage and subscription records at RevenueCat. You can do it in two ways:
    • In the app (instant): Settings › Privacy & Account › Delete Account.
    • By email (without the app): write to smtechnologies.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my LinkSilo account", from the Google email address you signed in with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.
  • Data on your device is removed when you delete it, sign out (synced data), or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for up to 14 months. Our providers may keep data in routine backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use LinkSilo without an account, so your links never leave your device.
  • Export your data anytime in the app (Settings › Export, JSON or CSV).
  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices (where available).
  • Delete your account and data as described above.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to certain processing. Email us at smtechnologies.dev@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

10. Children

LinkSilo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. For questions or privacy requests, email smtechnologies.dev@gmail.com.
