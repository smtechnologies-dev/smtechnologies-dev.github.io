1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy Uninstaller Pro. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure mobile experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you use our mobile application.

2. No Personal Data Collection

Easy Uninstaller Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or information to external servers. Your app preferences, theme settings, and favorite app selections are stored strictly locally on your own device's internal storage.

We do not have access to your personal files, usage history, or any other sensitive data. Your device data belongs entirely to you.

3. Device Permissions Declared

To deliver our core uninstallation and app management features, Easy Uninstaller Pro requires access to certain system permissions. Below is an explanation of why each permission is required:

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Used exclusively to scan and display the list of applications currently installed on your device so you can manage, sort, or uninstall them. We do not transmit this list off your device.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: Required to prompt the Android system's native uninstall dialog when you choose to remove an application.

To keep the application free for our users, Easy Uninstaller Pro incorporates Google AdMob advertising services. Google AdMob may collect and process certain identifiers of your device. This data is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies.

Data potentially processed by Google AdMob includes:

  • IP address and geolocation (for basic region-level target matching).
  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google AD_ID on Android, IDFA on iOS).
  • App performance and crash logs.

This data is used solely to prevent fraud, serve relevant advertisements, and deliver analytic usage stats. You can opt out of personalized ad tracking via your Android's Google Settings ➡️ Ads.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all your preferences and settings are stored locally on your device, you have complete ownership and control over your data. All saved configurations, favorite apps list, and visual preferences are automatically and permanently deleted from your system immediately upon uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has shared information, please contact us so we can verify and delete any local caching.
