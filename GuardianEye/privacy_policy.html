Roomi: Hidden Camera Detector

Privacy Policy — Last Updated: October 5, 2026

✅ Your scan results — camera view, magnetic readings, WiFi and Bluetooth devices — are processed on your device and never uploaded. The app uses Google services (AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) that collect limited device and usage data, described in sections 4 and 5.

1. Introduction

Roomi: Hidden Camera Detector ("Roomi," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information our app accesses, why it is needed, and how it is handled. By using Roomi, you agree to the terms described here.

2. Scan Data (Processed on Your Device Only)

Magnetometer / Sensor Data: Roomi reads your device's magnetic field sensor to detect anomalies that may indicate hidden electronic devices. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Camera: The Lens Detection and IR Scan features use your device camera to analyze the physical environment in real time. No photos or videos are ever captured, saved, or transmitted.

WiFi Network Information: The WiFi scanning feature reads the list of devices on your local network to identify potentially suspicious hardware. This information never leaves your device.

Bluetooth: The Bluetooth scanning feature detects nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices that may indicate hidden cameras or trackers. All scanning results are shown locally and are not stored or shared.

Location Permission: On Android, scanning for WiFi and Bluetooth networks requires the Location permission as mandated by the Android operating system. Roomi does not collect, store, or use your precise GPS location for any purpose.

3. Calibration Data

The magnetic baseline value set during calibration is saved locally on your device using secure local storage. This data is used only to improve detection accuracy and can be deleted at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & Data → Reset Calibration. Your app settings, scan counters and any Room Check reports you save (including the room name you type) are also stored only on your device. They are only shared if you tap Share yourself, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Roomi uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob collects and shares with Google your device's advertising ID, app interaction and ad interaction data, diagnostic information, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. This processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & Data → Ad Privacy Choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

Roomi uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example: which detection tool was opened, whether a scan finished, how many devices a scan found, app opens, and ad events) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (device model, OS version, app state and stack traces). These services collect an app instance identifier and device information and process it on Google's servers. We do not send scan contents such as device names, MAC/IP addresses, camera images or sensor readings to these services.

Analytics data is retained according to Firebase's default retention settings. We do not sell your data.

6. Data Security

Roomi does not operate its own servers or databases. Data collected by Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under Google's security practices. You can request deletion of data associated with your app instance by contacting us (section 9).

7. Children's Privacy

Roomi is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
