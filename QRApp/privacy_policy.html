Privacy Policy — Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Safe QR Code Scanner & Maker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information our app accesses, why it is needed, and how it is handled. By using our App, you agree to the terms described here.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

Scanning, decoding, and QR code generation happen on your device. We do not operate servers, and we do not receive your scans or created codes.

  • Camera: Used only to read QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera frames are analyzed on your device and are not saved or transmitted.
  • Images you choose: When you scan from an image, you pick it with the Android photo picker. The App only receives the image you select, decodes it on your device, and does not upload it. The App does not request access to your whole photo library.
  • Your brand kit: If you add a business name or logo for printable signs, it is stored only on your device and is used only to draw your QR codes and signs.
  • Saving QR codes: If you save a generated QR code, the App writes that image to your photo library (on older Android versions this requires storage permission).
  • Scan and creation history: Your scanned and created codes (which may include links, Wi-Fi names and passwords, or contact details) are stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. This storage is not encrypted by the App and is not backed up to the cloud by the App. It is deleted when you clear history in Settings or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads (such as banner, interstitial, and app-open ads) served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), app interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. This processing is governed by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used, for example which screens are opened and how often, so we can fix problems and improve features. Firebase may collect an app-instance identifier, device model and OS version, app version, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app usage events. We do not send the contents of your scans or created codes to Firebase. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

The App also uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings (such as how often ads appear) without an app update. It uses an app-installation identifier and your approximate region (country and time zone), and does not use your scans.

5. Data Security and Your Choices

Data sent by the Google SDKs above is encrypted in transit. Your scan history stays on your device and you control it: delete individual items in History, or everything with "Clear History" in Settings. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children.
