Privacy Policy

Note Prompt: Pin Notes & Lists · Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Note Prompt (listed on Google Play as Note Prompt: Pin Notes & Lists, "the app") handles information. The app lets you write notes and checklists and pin them to your device's notification bar.

In short: your notes stay on your device. The app has no account system and no server that receives your notes. Like most free apps, it uses Google services for advertising, analytics and configuration, which are described below.

2. Your Notes Stay on Your Device

Your notes, checklists, tags, colors, icons and image attachments are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not upload them, and we cannot read them.

A note leaves the app only when you choose to share or copy it yourself, for example with the Share button. In that case it goes to the app you pick through Android's share sheet.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

The app does not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details. However, the following Google services included in the app collect technical information from your device:

Google AdMob (advertising)

Shows ads that keep the app free. AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating system information, and how you interact with ads. If you consent, ads may be personalized; otherwise they are not.

Firebase Analytics

Helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. It records events such as which screen is opened, that a note was created or pinned (never the note's content), how often the app is opened, and device, app version and approximate region information. Firebase assigns an app-instance identifier to your installation.

Firebase Remote Config

Downloads app settings (for example, how often ads may appear) from Google's servers. This involves your IP address and the Firebase installation identifier.

None of these services receive the text of your notes, your checklists or your images. Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

4. Advertising Choices and Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where applicable).

You can also control ad personalization on your device under Android Settings → Google → Ads, where you can opt out of ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID.

5. Permissions the App Uses

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Needed to show your pinned notes and checklists in the notification bar. The app asks for this permission the first time you pin a note.

Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)

Lets the app show your pinned notes again after your phone restarts or the app is updated, because Android clears notifications at those moments. No data is sent anywhere.

Internet (INTERNET)

Used only by the advertising, analytics and configuration services described in section 3. Your notes are not sent over the internet.

Vibrate (VIBRATE)

Used when a one-time note alert is shown.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or phone number, and it does not use foreground services.

6. Photos You Attach

If you attach a photo to a note, you choose it through Android's photo picker. The app makes a reduced-size copy in its own private storage and uses it for that note. The photo is not uploaded or shared by the app. If you remove the photo or delete the note, the copy is deleted.

7. Data Retention, Backup and Deletion

You control your notes. You can delete any note, or all archived notes, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored notes, images and settings from your device.

Android may include app data in your Google account's device backup if you have backup turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is managed by Google and your own device settings.

Analytics and advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google's retention settings. Because we do not keep a user account or any personal profile, we cannot look up or delete data about one particular person on our side; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. The app keeps your notes only on your device, where you control them. For data processed by Google services, you can use the controls in section 4 or Google's own privacy tools, and you can contact us with any request.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. We recommend you review this page periodically.
