Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Note Prompt. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure mobile experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you use our mobile application.

2. No Personal Data Collection

Note Prompt does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or information to external servers. Your notes, checklists, task items, image attachments, and customizable color selections are stored strictly locally on your own device's internal storage.

We do not have access to your written notes, checklists, or saved images. Your notes belong entirely to you.

3. Device Permissions Declared

To deliver advanced reminders and background sticky note alerts, Note Prompt requires access to certain system permissions. Below is an explanation of why each permission is required:

⏰ Exact Alarm Permission (USE_EXACT_ALARM)

Used to schedule and trigger user-defined alarms at the exact specified hour. This ensures your note alerts appear precisely when you expect them.

🔔 Post Notifications Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to render customizable note reminders in your device's drawer and trigger notifications when an alarm fires.

⚙️ Foreground Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_REMINDER)

Required to maintain persistent "Sticky Notifications" inside the notification tray, allowing users to interact, tick items off checklists, or tap "Done" easily without service interruption.

4. Third-Party Services (AdMob Advertising)

To keep the application free for our users, Note Prompt incorporates Google AdMob advertising services. Google AdMob may collect and process certain identifiers of your device. This data is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies.

Data potentially processed by Google AdMob includes:

  • IP address and geolocation (for basic region-level target matching).
  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google AD_ID on Android, IDFA on iOS).
  • App performance and crash logs.

This data is used solely to prevent fraud, serve relevant advertisements, and deliver analytic usage stats. You can opt out of personalized ad tracking via your Android's Google Settings ➡️ Ads.

5. Image and Local File Storage

When you attach images to your notes (NotePrompt Pro feature), the files are processed using your system's native Media Picker and duplicated into our app's secure private documents directory. These images are not uploaded, shared, or indexed by our servers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all your notes are stored locally on your device, you have complete ownership and control over your data. All notes, checklists, images, and preferences are automatically and permanently deleted from your system immediately upon uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has shared information, please contact us so we can verify and delete any local caching.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated directly in this document. We advise you to review this page periodically for any modifications.
